# Ideas repo

This repo holds Boop's personal ideas list in `ideas.md`. None of the ideas are urgent and none have deadlines or priorities. The list exists so ideas don't get forgotten, and the monthly check-in exists to re-spark interest. It must never turn into bureaucracy that steals time from the ideas themselves or from life.

## Format rules

- Each idea is one bullet: `- **Name**: short note. _Last touched: YYYY-MM_`. Keep notes short and in Boop's own words.
- Ideas are loosely grouped under headings by kind. Move or add a group only if something clearly doesn't fit.
- No priorities, statuses, tags, estimates, due dates or templates. Don't add new fields.
- Never hard-wrap lines. Every paragraph or bullet is one line, however long.
- `## Check-in log` at the bottom gets one line per check-in. Once it passes about 12 lines, roll the older ones into a single summary line.

## Adding or updating an idea

When Boop mentions a new idea or progress on an existing one, add the idea or edit its note, set `Last touched` to the current month and commit with a short message such as `add: typing trainer game` or `update: well game`. Don't ask follow-up questions unless something is genuinely unclear.

## Monthly check-in

1. Read `ideas.md`.
2. Show Boop the **full list** in its groups, one line per idea, with when it was last touched.
3. **Highlight a couple of ideas** with whatever comes to mind about them: a connection between two ideas, a fun angle, a related thing that exists in the world, a question, or a note that it's been quiet for a long time. Use the check-in log to rotate, so the same ideas don't get highlighted every month. Don't suggest "first steps" or next actions. Each idea already has its own way of progressing and Boop decides that.
4. Ask briefly whether anything moved, sparked or should be dropped. Keep it light and conversational, with no interrogation item by item.
5. If Boop replies, update the affected notes and `Last touched` dates, add any new ideas and remove any he drops. Then add the log line and commit and push. If he doesn't reply, just add the log line saying what was highlighted, and commit.
